## Frozen evidence boundary

The footage directly proves:

- a spiral-bound School Memories Album;
- visible sections from Preschool/Kindergarten through later grades, including readable 9th and 11th Grade states;
- school-picture and self-drawing spaces;
- repeated prompts for name/signature, height, weight, teacher, friends, favorites, subjects, activities and reflections;
- a colorful cover and a substantial, varied page block.

It does **not** prove a child's reaction, parent-child interaction, a filled album, gift use, long-term preservation performance, exact full grade range/page count, price, offer, availability or a directional purchase action.

## Arm A — Old Director baseline

Arm A is a competent conventional product tour:

`cover identity → open book → early-grade prompts → photo/drawing spaces → later grades → thickness/cover Hero → generic soft CTA`

Its primary strategy is Information with Visual secondary. It correctly avoids unsupported claims and shows the real page structure, but the organizing logic remains close to the physical page order. The first screen says what the object is; the stronger reason to buy—one structured record spanning multiple school years—arrives later and is not the controlling story decision.

## Arm B — Experimental v2

Arm B selects:

- Primary: `information_led`
- Secondary: `emotion_led`
- Confidence: `high` for evidence coverage, not conversion probability

Its opening hypothesis is:

> One structured album can hold school memories from early years into later grades.

The plan begins with a supported early-grade/later-grade contrast, then identifies the product, proves grade progression, proves recurring photo/profile/reflection structure, translates that structure into restrained keepsake value, and ends with a Hero that summarizes “multiple school-year snapshots in one album.”

Emotion stays secondary because the footage contains no child, family, filled page or reaction. The permitted emotional layer is the possibility of keeping school-year details together for later reference. Claims about love, bonding, delight or guaranteed preservation are forbidden.

## Key difference

The largest difference is not wording. It is the unit of organization:

- Old Director: “What page/product view should come next?”
- Experimental v2: “What does the buyer need to understand next, and which source state can prove it?”

That difference changes the Hook, the role of the cover, the number of grade pages retained, caption duties, Hero meaning and fallback behavior.

## Primary-strategy decision

`Information primary + Emotion secondary` is the evidence-safe human commercial judgment.

Emotion demand is real, but direct emotional supply is weak. Making Emotion primary would force the plan to rely on missing people, completed-use or reaction footage. Information is both highly relevant to the purchase and strongly supplied by the material.

## Verdict and next gate

Experimental v2 is more aligned with the manual commercial reference judgment and is worth taking into a **separate controlled Cutter validation**. The next test should freeze both Director plans and ask whether the v2 timeline makes the one-album/multiple-years meaning understandable earlier and reduces meaningful re-edit preference.

This checkpoint does not start that test. It does not authorize production use or rule promotion. A formal blinded multi-editor plan review was not performed, so the current decision remains provisional at the Director-judgment layer.
