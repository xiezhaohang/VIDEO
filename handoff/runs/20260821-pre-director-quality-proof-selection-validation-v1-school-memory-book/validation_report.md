## Media Quality Gate findings

Three proposed intervals fail admission. The S09 Hook entry starts while focus and framing are still settling. The S02 memory-space entry includes page-search/settling before the evidence becomes readable. The S11 edge flip is dominated by rapid, unreadable page motion and hand searching; it is preparation-like handling, not a stable commercial proof.

S09 and S02 are recoverable by later in-points in the same sources and must be evaluated as new intervals. S11 is excluded. Task-relevant page-turn motion remains permitted in S06 and S10 because readable endpoints complete declared commercial tasks. Hand presence is not automatically rejected; it becomes disqualifying only when it blocks the required confirmation or makes handling—not the product—the dominant event.

## Proof Selection result

Two quality-passed proofs are not worth showing:

- S07 repeatable later-grade fields: true and readable, but the early/later Hook already exposes recurring fields and S06 proves section repetition. It adds detail, not a new buying reason.
- S08 favorites/reflection breadth: also true, but it is a secondary prompt category with tight framing and weak incremental purchase impact after the organizing mechanism is understood.

The selected minimum set preserves the frozen Director v2 logic: early-to-later-year Hook, product identity, separated grade sections, picture/drawing spaces and a complete-product Hero.

## Timeline difference

The first purchase meaning remains at 1.40 seconds. The S09 and S02 entries move to stable readable points. S07 and S08 are removed for low marginal proof value; S11 is removed for quality. The Hero therefore begins around 9.68 seconds instead of 17.58 seconds. Planned duration falls by 7.91 seconds while no unsupported claim is introduced.

The information-state count falls from 8 to 5. This is intentional compression, not an unconditional improvement: the core purchase meaning survives, but some perceived breadth is lost.

## Experimental Director v3 decision

The two layers are **worth entering Experimental Director v3 as an isolated pre-Director/candidate-selection experiment**, not as production rules. The validation directly corrects the observed eligibility gap and cleanly separates “what to say” from “which evidence is good enough to show.”

Promotion should remain blocked until a rendered New arm is reviewed against the frozen baseline. Preference risk is medium: aggressive proof compression may feel abrupt or undersell breadth, and no blinded human preference has been collected.
